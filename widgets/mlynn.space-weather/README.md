# Space weather

The planetary Kp index (0-9, with the last three days as a sparkline), solar wind speed and Bz, and today's NOAA radio blackout / solar radiation / geomagnetic storm scales. Kp 5+ is a geomagnetic storm, when the upper atmosphere heats up and drag on low-orbit satellites rises.

Data: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, `services.swpc.noaa.gov` (public, no key). Kp every 30 min, solar wind every 10 min.

Screenshot is a mock-up rendered from live data, not a TV capture.
